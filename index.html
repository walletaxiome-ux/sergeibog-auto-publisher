Platform Features

The platform is designed to simplify repetitive video publishing tasks while keeping users in control of their content and connected accounts.

Video Management

Organize video files and prepare publication metadata such as titles, descriptions and hashtags.

Publication Scheduling

Manage planned publication times and organize content into a structured publishing workflow.

Connected Accounts

Manage authorized social media connections used for publishing workflows requested by the user.

Content Queue

Review prepared videos and monitor their publication state before authorized publishing actions.

Metadata Management

Prepare titles, descriptions and hashtags together with each selected video.

Publishing Workflow

Move content through preparation, authorization, scheduling and publishing stages.

How It Works

SERGEIBOG AUTO PUBLISHER is designed around a simple, user-controlled publishing process.

01

Prepare Content

The user selects video content and prepares the publication information required for the selected publishing workflow.

02

Connect an Account

The user authorizes a supported social platform when an integration requires account access.

03

Manage the Workflow

The user manages publication status, scheduling and content information through the platform.

04

Publish

Authorized publishing actions are performed using the connected platform integration.

TikTok Integration

SERGEIBOG AUTO PUBLISHER can integrate with TikTok to support authorized publishing workflows. Users initiate the connection and grant permissions through the official TikTok authorization flow.

Login Kit

Users can authorize the application through the applicable TikTok authorization process.

Content Posting API

Authorized workflows can prepare video content for publishing through the applicable TikTok API.

Publishing Options

Depending on approved permissions and enabled capabilities, content can be prepared for direct posting or draft workflows.
