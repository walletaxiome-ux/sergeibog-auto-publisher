Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SERGEIBOG AUTO PUBLISHER handles information when users connect supported third-party services and use video publishing features.

2. Information We May Receive

When a user connects supported platforms, the application may receive basic account information and authorization data required to provide requested functionality.

3. TikTok Information

When users authorize TikTok integration, the application may receive information permitted by the scopes granted by the user through the official TikTok authorization process.

4. TikTok API Permissions

SERGEIBOG AUTO PUBLISHER may request permissions required for enabled features, including profile information, account statistics, video list access and video publishing capabilities.

Permissions are requested only through official TikTok authorization flows and are controlled by the user.

5. Content Data

The application may process videos, titles, descriptions, hashtags and other content selected by the user for publishing workflows.

6. Use of Information

Information is used to authenticate users, manage connected accounts, operate publishing workflows and provide requested functionality.

7. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms when necessary to provide features requested and authorized by the user.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party services, including TikTok, operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide application functionality or meet legal obligations.
